My 3 favorite All Time Movies

Best movies ever according to my-self

Troy

Epic historical war film inspired by Homer's Iliad. Set in ancient Greece, it follows the legendary conflict between the Greeks and the Trojans after the Trojan prince Paris falls in love with Helen, the wife of the Spartan king Menelaus, and takes her to Troy.
The story centers on the Greek warrior Achilles, whose unmatched skill in battle is tested as the Greeks lay siege to Troy. Themes of honor, pride, love, revenge, and the pursuit of everlasting glory drive the characters toward a tragic and unforgettable conclusion.
Starring Brad Pitt as Achilles, Eric Bana as Hector, Orlando Bloom as Paris, and Diane Kruger as Helen, Troy combines large-scale battle scenes with personal conflicts, offering a dramatic retelling of one of history's most famous legends.

Schindler's list

Historical drama based on the true story of Oskar Schindler, a German businessman who saved more than 1,100 Jewish people during the Holocaust by employing them in his factories and protecting them from Nazi persecution.
Set during World War II in Nazi-occupied Poland, the film follows Schindler's transformation from a profit-driven entrepreneur into a courageous humanitarian who risks his wealth and life to save others. Directed by Steven Spielberg and starring Liam Neeson, the film explores themes of compassion, moral courage, sacrifice, and the impact one individual can have in the face of unimaginable evil.
Widely regarded as one of the greatest films ever made, Schindler's List is both a powerful historical account and a deeply moving tribute to the resilience of the human spirit.

Die hard

Classic action thriller that follows John McClane, a New York City police officer who becomes trapped in a Los Angeles skyscraper after a group of heavily armed criminals seize the building during a Christmas Eve office party.
As the hostage situation unfolds, McClane must rely on his ingenuity, determination, and police skills to outsmart the criminals and their ruthless leader, Hans Gruber, while trying to rescue his estranged wife and the other hostages.
Starring Bruce Willis and Alan Rickman, Die Hard is renowned for its suspenseful action, memorable one-liners, and charismatic performances. It is widely considered one of the greatest action films ever made and helped redefine the genre for decades to come.
